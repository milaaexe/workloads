What is Slime Rancher?

Slime Rancher is a farming and adventure game where players explore a colorful world filled with adorable creatures called slimes. Players take on the role of Beatrix LeBeau, who travels to the Far, Far Range to start her own ranch.

One of my favorite things about Slime Rancher is collecting different slimes and discovering new areas. Each slime has its own appearance, behaviors, and favorite foods. I also enjoy decorating my ranch and exploring the different environments throughout the game.
